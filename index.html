OurFlow was built around a simple idea: important details are easy to forget, especially when you're focused on getting somewhere, attending an appointment, or managing a busy day. No complicated menus. No unnecessary steps. Just a simple way to remember what matters. 🧭

🧭 To begin, type: "Start journey to [destination]"

OurFlow helps you remember what matters during your journey and recall it when you need it.



OurFlow uses artificial intelligence (AI) to help you save, organize, and recall important journey information.

Responses are generated from available information and may not always be accurate. Always verify important details before relying on them. 🧭

OurFlow helps users save, organize, recall, and summarize information during a journey, including locations, parking, appointments, medications, questions, instructions, photos, notes, and other user-provided details.

Responses are generated using AI and may occasionally be incomplete, incorrect, outdated, or require additional information.

When information is limited, OurFlow may ask follow-up questions before providing a response.

Users should independently verify important information, including medical, legal, financial, transportation, travel, safety, and appointment-related decisions.

Anonymous usage analytics may be collected to improve app performance, reliability, and user experience.

OurFlow is not an emergency, safety, legal, medical, mental health, crisis, or professional advisory service.

Please use your own judgment and seek assistance from qualified professionals, businesses, service providers, local authorities, or emergency services when appropriate. 🧭
